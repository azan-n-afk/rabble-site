Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026

The short version: Rabble runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, and no tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains how Rabble ("the app", "we", "us", or "our"), published by AEA Solutions Ltd, handles your information. AEA Solutions Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales and is the data controller responsible for the app.

1. Information we collect

Rabble does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not require or offer accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.
  • We do not transmit any information from your device to us or to any third party.

Anything you do in the app, such as the players you add or the content you create during a game, stays on your device and is not sent anywhere. This information is temporary and exists only for the duration of your play session.

2. How the app works

Rabble is a "pass-and-play" party game designed to be played by passing a single device between players in the same room. All game content and logic runs locally on your device. The app does not need an internet connection to function and does not communicate with any server operated by us.

3. Third-party services

Rabble does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. The app is distributed through the Apple App Store; your download and any interactions with the App Store itself are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not ours.

4. Children and mature content

Rabble contains some content intended for adult players. This content is hidden behind an optional toggle that is turned off by default, so the app can also be enjoyed by younger players in its standard mode. The app is age-rated on the App Store to reflect the presence of this optional mature content. Because we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

5. Your rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have rights over your personal data, including the rights of access, correction, and erasure. Because Rabble collects and stores no personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about your rights, you can contact us using the details below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features that change how the app handles information. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and where appropriate we will provide a more prominent notice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
