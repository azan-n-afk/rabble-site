A pass-and-play party games app for iOS. Pass the phone, play together, get the whole room laughing.

What is Rabble?

Rabble is a collection of party games you play together on a single device, passing the phone around the room. From quick guessing games to spicy social showdowns, it is built for getting a group laughing. Some content is intended for adult players and is hidden behind an optional toggle, off by default.
